Camera Motion Alert Troubleshooting

Motion alerts may occur when the camera detects movement in its field of view.

To reduce false motion alerts:
1. Check the camera position.
2. Make sure the camera is not pointed toward moving objects.
3. Check the motion detection settings.
4. Verify that the firmware is up to date.

Rollover

Rollover allows unused subscription data to be carried forward according to the applicable subscription plan.

If a customer reports that rollover did not occur, verify the customer's subscription and check the latest rollover event.
